How the assessment works. LAN6 is a receptive-vocabulary task: the child hears a word and taps the matching picture from four choices (the practice item p01 uses two choices). Items are organized by target age band — 2YO (q01–q08), 3YO (q09–q16), 4YO (q17–q24), and KG (q25 onward).

About the Exits column. The raw export reports the last item a child saw. Because a child who left after seeing item X actually exited the app when the next item appeared, every value has been shifted down by one position along the actual administration order (). Exits for the first scored item are therefore attributed from the practice item p01, and the children whose last item was are counted as having completed the assessment rather than as exits.
